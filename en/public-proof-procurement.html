Standard 8-12 week validation pilot

1. Baseline

Measure the pre-adoption state using real operational data: unresolved actions, overdue actions, action time and evidence completeness.

2. Operational Validation

Run the real workflow at one or more sites from hazard reporting through human approval, action, evidence and reassessment.

3. Evidence Review

Review before/after KPIs with sample size, period, source data and evidence hashes in a pilot outcome report.

No field sample means no performance claim. Synthetic data is for product demonstration only and is never used to claim public-sector pilot outcomes.

Institution-grade KPIs

Execution

Assignment time, remediation time, on-time closure rate and overdue rate.

Evidence

Before/after evidence completeness, residual-risk reassessment and final human approval.

Learning & recurrence

Human correction of AI candidates, recurrent hazards and site/contractor patterns.

Public SaaS readiness principles

Security & access

Role-based access, audit logs, incident response and evidence for vulnerability and change management.

Continuity & SLA

Measured availability, detection/response/recovery, RTO/RPO and recovery-test evidence.

Privacy & data

Purpose, retention, deletion, return/export, lawful basis, provenance and separate authorization for AI training use.
