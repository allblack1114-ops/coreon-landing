How to read

Status definitions

The Enforcement Decree of the Serious Accidents Punishment Act (SAPA) requires a review "at least once every half-year" for most obligations, including hazard improvement, hearing workers' opinions and contractor management. What COREON records is the basis for that review: event-level evidence that an improvement was actually carried out and confirmed by an authorized person.

Supported

The current production product creates event-level execution records and evidence for the obligation.

Partial

Part of the action and evidence record is provided. The remaining procedure is performed by the business or another system.

Customer-performed / Roadmap

Not a current product capability. The business performs it directly; some items are managed as roadmap candidates.

Rating principle. Providing a form or checklist alone is not rated “Supported”. An item is rated “Supported” only when owner assignment, before/after evidence and an authorized approval record are actually created. This table is updated when the law or the product changes.

2026 Law Updates

Regulatory changes in 2026

The regulatory focus is shifting from "was an assessment done?" to "was the improvement implemented and verified?"

Effective 2026-06-01

OSH Act Art. 36 risk assessment amended

The employer's duty to implement improvement measures and the duty to involve and inform workers were strengthened.

Ministry of Employment and Labor, Cheongju (KR) ↗
Phased from 2027-01-01

Fines for risk-assessment violations

Applies from 2027-01-01 to workplaces with 50 or more regular workers (construction: projects of KRW 5 billion or more), and from 2028-01-01 to smaller ones; up to KRW 10 million per the official notice.

MOEL Goyang (KR) ↗ · MOEL Incheon North (KR) ↗
Passed 2026-10-01

Penalty surcharge for repeated fatalities

A surcharge of up to 5% of operating profit for companies with three or more work-related deaths in a year, plus an expanded right to stop work. The effective date must be confirmed after promulgation.

Maeil Business Newspaper (KR) ↗

Boundary

What COREON does and does not do

Principles enforced by the product

  • An event cannot close without required evidence, residual-risk reassessment and authorized approval.
  • History is kept under one event ID from field input to approval and closure.
  • AI only assists with classification, summaries and recommendations; final safety judgment and closure stay with authorized people.
  • Open, overdue and evidence-gap items needed for the half-year review can be checked per site.

Boundaries we deliberately keep

  • This mapping is not legal advice and does not guarantee legal compliance or exemption from liability.
  • Whether an obligation is met is decided by the business and its legal counsel.
  • Evidence hashes and audit trails do not automatically establish legal admissibility.
  • COREON does not guarantee zero accidents or any prevention outcome.
