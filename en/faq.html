FAQ

COREON Safety AX Agent

Key questions for enterprise, public-sector and field teams.

What is COREON Safety AX Agent?

An industrial-safety execution and evidence platform connecting hazard reporting, human review, ownership, corrective action, Evidence, residual-risk reassessment and authorized closure.

Does AI make the final safety decision?

No. AI assists classification, retrieval and organization. Final safety judgment, risk acceptance and closure remain with authorized people.

How is KOSHA public data used?

Official accident cases are used as review references and do not automatically change a customer risk score or final decision.

Can COREON start with a pilot?

Yes. A pilot can start with a limited site or workflow, defined baseline and KPIs, and real Safety Events before broader rollout.

How are customer boundaries handled?

Customer, site and role scopes are separated in application logic, with customer-specific commissioning and isolation verification during onboarding.

Is Vision Edge fully field-validated?

Vision Edge remains a pilot capability until independent real-field evidence is available.
