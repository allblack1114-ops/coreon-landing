AI / HUMAN AUTHORITY

Does AI make the final safety decision?

No. AI may assist classification, summarization and recommendations. Final safety judgment, risk acceptance and closure remain with authorized people. Fail-Closed rules block closure when required evidence, residual-risk review or approval is missing.
