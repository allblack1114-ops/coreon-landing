COREON SAFETY AVATAR · HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP

Connect safety training
to field judgment and action.

Safety Avatar uses scenario-based conversation to assess a worker's risk awareness and response, with KOSHA public safety references as grounding. Important findings never change official risk or create formal events automatically; authorized Human Review is required before operational use.

Not an isolated chatbot—an input interface to Safety AX.

Avatar conversations cannot automatically create official Safety Events or mutate customer risk values. Only findings that require operational action are moved through Human Review into COREON's corrective-action, evidence and residual-risk workflow.
