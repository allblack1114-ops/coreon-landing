Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026 · Previous version: effective August 1, 2025 · 한국어

COREON HOLDINGS Co., Ltd. (brand “COREON”, the “Company”) sets out this policy under the Korean Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA). It applies to the Company website (www.coreon-global.com) and the COREON Safety AX service (app.coreon-global.com, the “Service”). The Korean version prevails if the two versions differ.

1. Our role (controller and processor)

  • Website inquiries, Service account management, and free safety rooms opened by individuals: the Company is the controller.
  • Business or public-sector customers that enter data about their workers and contractors: the customer is the controller and the Company processes the data on the customer’s behalf as a processor, under the customer’s own privacy policy and the contract between the customer and the Company.

4. Provision to third parties

The Company provides personal information to third parties only where PIPA Articles 17 and 18 permit, such as with consent or as required by law. The Company does not currently provide personal information to third parties on a regular basis.

6. AI features

  • Photo review, classification and summaries use generative AI (OpenAI models). AI output is assistive: it lists hazard candidates and questions, and authorized people confirm safety status, restart of work and any legal judgment. See the AI notice.
  • AI photo review is instructed not to infer the identity or sensitive attributes of people.
  • The Company does not use customer Service data to train AI models without separate explicit consent.

7. Deletion

Personal information is deleted without delay when its retention period ends or its purpose is achieved. Electronic files are deleted so they cannot be restored; paper is shredded or incinerated. Records that must be kept by law or contract are kept separately with restricted access.

8. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, suspension of processing or withdrawal of consent at any time using the contact below. The Company acts without delay after identity verification. For data controlled by a customer (Section 1), please contact that customer; requests received by the Company are forwarded to the customer. Requests through an agent require a power of attorney.

9. Security measures

  • Administrative: minimum access rights, internal management rules
  • Technical: encrypted transport (HTTPS; HSTS on the Service), one-way hashed passwords, role- and site-based access separation, security headers (CSP and others), access and processing logs, regular backups
  • Physical: physical controls of processor (cloud provider) data centers

10. Cookies and external resources

The website does not currently use visitor analytics tools such as Google Analytics. The Service uses essential cookies for login and security; blocking them may prevent login. Some pages load fonts from an external CDN (jsDelivr). If analytics tools are introduced, this policy will be updated first.
