Why COREON

The largest safety-management gap starts after a hazard is found.

Finding and reporting risk does not change the safety state. The workflow must preserve who owns the action, when it is due, what changed, what evidence proves it, and what residual risk remains.

RESPONSIBILITY

RESIDUAL RISK

Reassess what risk remains after action

Canonical Safety Execution

Detect → Verify → Assign → Control → Prove → Reassess → Close

Simple for field users, strict inside the engine. One Safety Event keeps a consistent identity and evidence lineage from first report to closure.

01 DETECTDetectCapture hazards and near misses.
02 VERIFYVerifyHuman review of field context and references.
03 ASSIGNAssignSet accountable owner and deadline.
04 CONTROLControlPerform corrective or preventive action.
05 PROVEProveAttach evidence and review records.
06 REASSESSReassessHuman reassessment of residual risk.
07 CLOSECloseAuthorized closure only after required gates pass.
REOPEN · If new risk, failed control effectiveness or evidence-integrity problems appear after closure, the event is reopened for renewed review.
