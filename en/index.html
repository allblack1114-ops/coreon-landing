Customer Problem

The biggest safety gap often begins after a hazard is found.

Risk assessments and TBMs matter, but prevention breaks down when responsibility, due dates, evidence and follow-up are disconnected. COREON connects the final mile of safety execution.

01 · OWNERSHIP GAP

The hazard is known, but ownership and deadline are unclear.

Assign an accountable owner and due date to every controlled safety item.

02 · EVIDENCE GAP

An action is said to be complete, but before/after status is hard to verify.

Connect corrective-action evidence and approval history to the event.

03 · RECURRENCE GAP

Similar hazards recur without linking prior cases or residual risk.

Use KOSHA public safety references and internal history as reviewable context, with Human Review retained.
