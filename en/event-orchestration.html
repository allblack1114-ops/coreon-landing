COREON SAFETY AX AGENT · P1 (in production since 2026-10-06)

One event. Every follow-through, connected.

When a hazard or near-miss event is registered, COREON links not only safety actions but also work owned by other teams: training, equipment inspection, procurement, quality inspection, compliance review and escalated approval. The event cannot be closed while those conditions are unmet.

Supported in production

  • Cross-functional work linked to the event: safety action, training, equipment inspection, procurement, quality inspection, compliance review, escalated approval
  • Per-site profiles with independent approval
  • Typed follow-ups: evidence request, recapture, additional control, retraining, requote, reorder, reinspection, rework, site recheck, escalated approval
  • Evidence from follow-ups flows back into the parent work item
  • Completeness evaluation blocks closure when conditions are unmet
  • Residual risk calculated from the verified hierarchy of controls; administrative controls and PPE alone cannot bring residual risk below 50%
  • Human confirmation is bound to the inputs at the time of review; executive risk acceptance is supported
  • If supporting evidence is invalidated, the closed event is reopened
